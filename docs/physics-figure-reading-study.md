# Selected physics figures: a comparative study

Visual study dates: 15 September 2026. Scope: selected rendered figures,
captions and adjacent discussion in **20 distinct papers**. Thirteen form a
cross-field starting sample; seven were identified through an author-supplied
[INSPIRE record](https://inspirehep.net/authors/2778281). This is a purposive
sample, not a representative survey, ranking, scientific audit or full-paper
reading claim. Counts are separate from the prose-reading corpus.

The observations below concern communication, not endorsement of every physical
conclusion or historical graphic convention. PDF page numbers are one-based;
they can differ from printed article pages. No source figure is redistributed.

These preferences are useful examples, not a claim that every coauthor or every
figure has one identical style. Do not copy a label, unit, normalization or color
convention without checking its meaning in the new manuscript.

## Synthesis with exceptions

1. **Make the relationship visible.** Often the important decision is the
   observable, normalization or comparison, not decorative styling. A ratio
   helps expose a correction but should retain physical scale and uncertainty.
2. **Give related panels complementary jobs.** A schematic may explain a
   spectrum; a residual may qualify an observable. Repeated panels are useful
   for comparison, but not all papers need multipanel figures.
3. **Keep encodings stable and redundant.** Color, line style and marker shape
   should have a recoverable meaning. Historical examples also demonstrate
   why thin labels, dense legends and color-only distinctions need improvement.
4. **Preserve the evidence hierarchy.** A model curve, fit, bound, data point,
   hypothetical construction and confidence region cannot share ambiguous
   visual semantics. A clean curve does not certify convergence or novelty.
5. **Apply rather than endlessly expand the corpus.** Evaluate the next figure
   against its physical question and saved evidence. Add examples when a
   missing figure type warrants them, not to inflate a reading count.
